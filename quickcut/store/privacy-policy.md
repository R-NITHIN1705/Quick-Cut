# QuickCut Privacy Policy

QuickCut is designed to keep your captures on your device.

## Information you choose to save

When you start a capture, QuickCut reads the text or link you select so it can copy it and, if saving succeeds, add it to your capture history. The history and extension preferences are stored in Chrome's local extension storage on this device. You can remove individual captures or clear the history from QuickCut.

## Collection and sharing

QuickCut does not send captures or preferences to the developer or any server. It does not use analytics, advertising trackers, or remote code. QuickCut does not sell or share your information.

## Permissions

QuickCut uses temporary access to the active tab only after you invoke a capture action. It uses local storage for your history and settings, scripting to run the capture interaction you requested, and clipboard access to copy your chosen capture.

## Contact

For privacy questions, contact the extension developer through the support contact listed on the QuickCut Chrome Web Store page.
